2 CRICKET - LEICESTERSHIRE TAKE OVER AT TOP AFTER INNINGS VICTORY .  
3 LONDON 1996-08-30  
4 West Indian all-rounder Phil Simmons took four for 38 on Friday as Leicestershire beat Somerset by an innings and 39 runs in two days to take over at the head of the county championship .  
5 Their stay on top , though , may be short-lived as title rivals Essex , Derbyshire and Surrey all closed in on victory while Kent made up for lost time in their rain-affected match against Nottinghamshire .  
6 After bowling Somerset out for 83 on the opening morning at Grace Road , Leicestershire extended their first innings by 94 runs before being bowled out for 296 with England discard Andy Caddick taking three for 83 .  
7 Trailing by 213 , Somerset got a solid start to their second innings before Simmons stepped in to bundle them out for 174 .  
8 Essex , however , look certain to regain their top spot after Nasser Hussain and Peter Such gave them a firm grip on their match against Yorkshire at Headingley .  
9 Hussain , considered surplus to England 's one-day requirements , struck 158 , his first championship century of the season , as Essex reached 372 and took a first innings lead of 82 .  
10 By the close Yorkshire had turned that into a 37-run advantage but off-spinner Such had scuttled their hopes , taking four for 24 in 48 balls and leaving them hanging on 119 for five and praying for rain .  
11 At the Oval , Surrey captain Chris Lewis , another man dumped by England , continued to silence his critics as he followed his four for 45 on Thursday with 80 not out on Friday in the match against Warwickshire .  
12 He was well backed by England hopeful Mark Butcher who made 70 as Surrey closed on 429 for seven , a lead of 234 .  
13 Derbyshire kept up the hunt for their first championship title since 1936 by reducing Worcestershire to 133 for five in their second innings , still 100 runs away from avoiding an innings defeat .  
14 Australian Tom Moody took six for 82 but Chris Adams , 123 , and Tim O'Gorman , 109 , took Derbyshire to 471 and a first innings lead of 233 .  
15 After the frustration of seeing the opening day of their match badly affected by the weather , Kent stepped up a gear to dismiss Nottinghamshire for 214 .  
16 They were held up by a gritty 84 from Paul Johnson but ex-England fast bowler Martin McCague took four for 55 .  
17 By stumps Kent had reached 108 for three .

83 The world 's costliest footballer Alan Shearer was named as the new England captain on Friday .  
84 The 26-year-old , who joined Newcastle for 15 million pounds sterling ( $ 23.4 million ) , takes over from Tony Adams , who led the side during the European championship in June , and former captain David Platt .  
85 Adams and Platt are both injured and will miss England 's opening World Cup qualifier against Moldova on Sunday .  
86 Shearer takes the captaincy on a trial basis , but new coach Glenn Hoddle said he saw no reason why the former Blackburn and Southampton skipper should not make the post his own .  
87 " I 'm sure there wo n't be a problem , I 'm sure Alan is the man for the job , " Hoddle said .  
88 " There were three or four people who could have done it but when I spoke to Alan he was up for it and really wanted it .  
89 " In four days it 's very difficult to come to a 100 percent conclusion about something like this ...  
90 but he knows how to conduct himself , his team mates respect him and he knows about the team situation even though he plays up front . "  
91 Shearer 's Euro 96 striking partner Teddy Sheringham withdrew from the squad with an injury on Friday .  
92 He will probably be replaced by Shearer 's Newcastle team mate Les Ferdinand .

117 BOXING - PANAMA 'S ROBERTO DURAN FIGHTS THE SANDS OF TIME .  
118 PANAMA CITY 1996-08-30  
119 Panamanian boxing legend Roberto " Hands of Stone " Duran climbs into the ring on Saturday in another age-defying attempt to sustain his long career .  
120 Duran , 45 , takes on little-known Mexican Ariel Cruz , 30 , in a super middleweight non-title bout in Panama City .  
121 The fight , Duran 's first on home soil for 10 years , is being billed here as the " Return of the Legend " and Duran still talks as if he was in his prime .  
122 " I want a fifth title .  
123 This match is to prepare me .  
124 I feel good .  
125 I 'm not retiring , " Duran told Reuters .  
126 But those close to the boxer acknowledge that the man who has won championships in four different weight classes -- lightweight , welterweight , junior middleweight and middleweight -- is drawing close to the end of his career .  
127 " Each time he fights , he 's on the last frontier of his career .  
128 If he loses Saturday , it could devalue his position as one of the world 's great boxers , " Panamanian Boxing Association President Ramon Manzanares said .  
129 Duran , whose 97-12 record spans three decades , hopes a win in the 10-round bout will earn him a rematch against Puerto Rico 's Hector " Macho " Camacho .  
130 Camacho took a controversial points decision against the Panamanian in Atlantic City in June in a title fight .

294 TENNIS - TARANGO , O'BRIEN SPRING TWIN UPSETS UNDER THE LIGHTS .  
295 Larry Fine  
296 NEW YORK 1996-08-30  
297 Andre Agassi escaped disaster on Thursday but Wimbledon finalist MaliVai Washington and Marcelo Rios were not so fortunate on a night of upsets at the U.S. Open .  
298 The 11th-seeded Washington fell short of reprising his Wimbledon miracle comeback as he lost to red-hot wildcard Alex O'Brien 6-3 6-4 5-7 3-6 6-3 in a two hour 51 minute struggle on the Stadium court .  
299 Next door on the grandstand , 10th seed Rios lost to another player with a Wimbledon connection -- bad boy Jeff Tarango .  
300 The temperamental left-hander defeated the Chilean 6-4 4-6 7-6 6-2 .  
301 The day programme went smoothly although sixth-seeded former champion Agassi had to wriggle out of a dangerous 3-6 0-4 hole , winning 18 of the last 19 games against India 's Leander Paes .  
302 But the night belonged to the upstarts .  
303 Washington , who climbed back from a 1-5 deficit , two sets down in the third set against Todd Martin in the Wimbledon semifinals , looked poised for another sensational comeback .  
304 O'Brien , a winner two weeks ago in New Haven for his first pro title , served for the match at 5-4 in the third set before Washington came charging back .  
305 " I just kept saying to myself , ' keep giving yourself the best chance to win , keep battling , maybe something will happen , ' " said the 26-year-old O'Brien , ranked 65th .  
306 " I kept my composure and I was proud of myself for that -- usually I would have folded up the tent and gone home . "  
307 The hard-serving O'Brien , a former U.S. collegiate national champion , fired up 17 aces to ultimately subdue the never-say-die Washington .  
308 The fifth set stayed on serve until the sixth game , when Washington , after saving one break point with a forehand winner down the line , netted a backhand to give O'Brien a 4-2 lead .  
309 The Texan blasted in two aces to hold serve at 5-2 and then converted his eighth match point for victory when Washington found the net with another backhand from 40-0 .  
310 " You just kind of keep fighting and you keep trying to make him play a little bit .  
311 I think he got a little tight at a couple of moments , " said Washington . "  
312 But I think he served pretty well when he had to . "  
313 Tarango , whose Wimbledon tantrum two years ago brought him a $ 28,000 fine and suspension from this year 's tournament at the All-England Club , argued calls and taunted fans in his lively two hour , 24 minute tango with Rios on the grandstand .  
314 A boisterous cheering section backed the distracted Chilean and booed the lanky American , who ate up all the attention .  
315 " I 'm an emotional player , " said the 104th-ranked Tarango . "  
316 I think I played very well tonight , very focused . "  
317 The match turned on the third-set tiebreaker , which the American won 7-5 much to the dismay of the spectators .  
318 " I love the crowd if they boo me every day .  
319 It fires me up , makes me play my best tennis , " Tarango said .  
320 " I played some of my best tennis in college when fraternities were throwing beer on me .  
321 If tennis was like that every day , I think everybody wold be having a lot more fun . "  
322 Rios did not appreciate Tarango 's antics .  
323 " He 's always complaining too much , " said Rios . "  
324 But I think it 's not that .  
325 I think I played really bad .  
326 It was tough to play at night .  
327 Balls were going really fast .  
328 I lost too many points that I never lose .  
329 I did n't play my tennis . "  
330 " I do n't see the ball like I see during the day .  
331 I play an American so that 's why I play at night .  
332 I did n't feel good on the court . "  
333 At the end of the match , Tarango blew sarcastic kisses to the crowd , then jiggled his body to a Rios rooting section in a jeering salute .  
334 " I support their enthusiasm , " Tarango said about the fans . "  
335 At the same time , they 're cheering blatantly against me .  
336 After I won I figured I could give them a little razzle-dazzle . "

368 Nolan Henke fired a nine-under-par 62 to grab a two-shot lead after the opening round of the $ 1.2 million Greater Milwaukee Open Thursday as 20-year-old Tiger Woods shot 67 in his professional debut .  
369 Henke stood two strokes ahead of Bob Estes and three up on Billy Andrade , Duffy Waldorf and Jesper Parnevik .  
370 Woods , who turned pro Tuesday after winning an unprecedented third successive U.S. Amateur Championship , almost eagled the 18th hole .  
371 He settled for a birdie and a four-under opening round that left him five shots off the pace .  
372 " Yesterday was the toughest day I 've had for a long time , " Woods said . "  
373 Today , I got to play golf . "  
374 He added : " I thought I got off off to a great start .  
375 It was a perfect start .  
376 I 'm in a good position . "  
377 Henke , who called his round a " pleasant surprise , " finished with six birdies on the final eight holes .  
378 " We finally got things going in the right direction , " he said . "  
379 It was my best round in a very long time .  
380 My short game has improved since I 've had to use it so often .  
381 That 's always been the worst part of my game .  
382 All in all , playing bad 's been a good experience . "  
383 Henke , who came within one shot of the course record set by Andrew Magee during Wednesday 's pro-am , has three career PGA Tour victories , but none since the 1993 BellSouth Classic .  
384 Estes , whose only win came at the 1994 Texas Open and whose best finish this year was a third-place tie at the Nortel Open in January , eagled the par-five fourth hole and added five birdies to grab sole possession of second place .  
385 " No bogeys on the card , " he noted . "  
386 Sometimes I take more pride in that . "  
387 Woods was among a group of 13 players at four under , including 1993 champion Billy Mayfair , who tied for second at last week 's World Series of Golf , and former U.S. Open champ Payne Stewart .  
388 Defending champion Scott Hoch shot a three-under 68 and was six strokes back .  
389 Phil Mickelson , the only four-time winner on the PGA Tour , skipped the tournament after winning the World Series of Golf last week .  
390 Mark Brooks , Tom Lehman and Mark O'Meara , who make up the rest of the top four on the money list , also took the week off .

400 BERLIN 1996-08-30  
401 American Dennis Mitchell outclassed Olympic 100 metres champion Donovan Bailey for the third time at a major post-Games meeting in front of the most experienced sprinting crowd in the world on Friday .  
402 Watched by an array of former Olympic sprint champions at the Berlin grand prix meeting , Mitchell made a brilliant start in the 100 metres and held off Bailey 's strong finish to win in 10.08 seconds despite cool conditions .  
403 Bailey , who set a world record of 9.84 on his way to victory in Atlanta , could not catch his American rival and had to settle for third in a tight finish .  
404 Jamaica 's Michael Green was second with 10.09 with Bailey finishing in 10.13 .  
405 Last Friday Mitchell , who finished fourth at the Atlanta Games , upstaged a trio of Olympic champions including Bailey to win the 100 in Brussels .  
406 Earlier this month he also beat world champion Bailey in Zurich .  
407 Berlin , Brussels and Zurich all belong to the most lucrative series in the sport , the Golden Four .  
408 Among the crowd on Friday were Olympic 100 metres champions going back to 1948 .  
409 They had been invited to the meeting to watch a special relay to mark the 60th anniversary of Jesse Owens 's four gold medals at the 1936 Olympics in the same Berlin stadium .  
410 " Today the concentration was the most important thing for me , " Mitchell said .  
411 Despite the coolish conditions American Olympic champion Gail Devers looked in commanding form in the women 's 100 , clocking 10.89 to defeat Jamaican rival Merlene Ottey , who was second in 10.94 .

582 SOCCER - THREE STANDARD LIEGE PLAYERS BANNED , CLUB FINED .  
583 GENEVA 1996-08-30  
584 UEFA came down heavily on Belgian club Standard Liege on Friday for " disgraceful behaviour " in an Intertoto final match against Karlsruhe of Germany .  
585 The Belgian club were fined 25,000 Swiss francs ( $ 20,850 ) for unsporting conduct and captain Guy Hellers banned for seven games .  
586 He was sent off for insulting the referee and then urged his team mates to protest .  
587 Roberto Bisconti will be sidelined for six Euro ties after pushing the referee in the back as he protested about a Karlsruhe goal , while Didier Ernst was banned for four matches for a verbal attack soon after Bisconti was also dismissed .  
588 Karlsruhe won the August 20 match 3-1 thanks to two late goals .  
589 They took the tie 3-2 on aggregate and qualified for the UEFA Cup .

591 ATHLETICS - HARRISON , EDWARDS TO MEET IN SARAJEVO .  
592 MONTE CARLO 1996-08-30  
593 Olympic champion Kenny Harrison and world record holder Jonathan Edwards will both take part in a triple jump competition at the Solidarity Meeting for Sarajevo on September 9 .  
594 The International Amateur Athletic Federation said on Friday that a schedule reshuffle had allowed organisers to hold a men 's triple jump as well as the women 's long jump on the " one usable runway at the war-devastated " Kosevo stadium .  
595 Atlanta Games silver medal winner Edwards has called on other leading athletes to take part in the Sarajevo meeting -- a goodwill gesture towards Bosnia as it recovers from the war in the Balkans -- two days after the grand prix final in Milan .  
596 Edwards was quoted as saying : " What type of character do we show by going to the IAAF Grand Prix Final in Milan where there is a lot of money to make but refusing to make the trip to Sarajevo as a humanitarian gesture ? "

604 SOCCER - MILAN 'S LENTINI MOVES TO ATALANTA .  
605 MILAN 1996-08-30  
606 Former Italian international winger Gianluigi Lentini , transferred to Milan in 1992 for what was believed to be a world record sum , has been loaned to serie A club Atalanta for a year , newspapers reported on Friday .  
607 The Gazzetta dello Sport said the deal would cost Atalanta around $ 600,000 .  
608 Lentini , 27 , joined Milan from Torino in a $ 12 million deal that many have speculated involved far more money changing hands and which has subsequently been investigated by magistrates for alleged financial irregularities .  
609 The player suffered severe head injuries in a near-fatal car crash the following year and has since struggled to regain the form that made him a hero in Turin .  
610 The move to Bergamo-based Atalanta reunites Lentini , who fell out with ex-Milan coach Fabio Capello last season , with his former coach at Torino , Emiliano Mondonico .
